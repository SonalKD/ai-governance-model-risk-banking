# Risk Assessment

## Overview
This section identifies potential risks associated with using an AI system for customer risk profiling in a CDD process.

---

## Key Risks

### 1. Data Quality Risk
If the input data (e.g., country, business activity) is incorrect or outdated, the AI system may produce incorrect risk ratings.

---

### 2. Bias Risk
The model may unfairly classify certain countries, industries, or customer profiles as higher risk without proper justification.

---

### 3. Explainability Risk
If the model cannot clearly explain why a customer is classified as high risk, it creates challenges for analysts, auditors, and regulators.

---

### 4. Over-reliance Risk
Analysts may rely too heavily on the AI output without proper review, leading to poor decision-making.

---

### 5. Regulatory Risk
Incorrect or non-transparent decisions may lead to non-compliance with regulatory expectations.

---

### 6. Model Drift Risk
Over time, the model may become less accurate due to changes in data patterns or risk environments.

---

### 7. Incorrect or Misleading Output Risk (Hallucination)

If the AI system generates incorrect or misleading explanations or risk scores, it may lead to wrong decision-making by analysts.

In cases where generative AI is used, the system may produce confident but incorrect reasoning (hallucination), which can mislead users and create regulatory and compliance risks.

This risk highlights the importance of validation, explainability, and human oversight.



## Summary
These risks highlight the need for strong governance, human oversight, and continuous monitoring of the AI system.
