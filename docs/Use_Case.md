# Use Case Definition

## Project Title
AI Governance & Model Risk Framework for AI-assisted CDD Risk Profiling

## Business Problem
CDD analysts often assess customer risk manually using information such as country, business activity, and ownership structure. This can be time-consuming and may lead to inconsistent assessments.

## Proposed AI Use Case
An AI system supports the analyst by suggesting a customer risk rating such as Low, Medium, or High based on selected inputs.

## Intended Users
CDD / KYC analysts

## Example Inputs
- Country of incorporation
- Business activity
- Ownership structure
- Customer profile information

## Example Output
Suggested customer risk rating with explanation

## Human Oversight
The final decision remains with the human analyst. The AI system only supports the decision-making process.

## Key Risks
- Incorrect or outdated data
- Bias in risk scoring
- Lack of explainability
- Over-reliance on AI by analysts
- Regulatory and compliance risk
